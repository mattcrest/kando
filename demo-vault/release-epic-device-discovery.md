# Device Discovery & Pairing

Automatic detection and onboarding of smart devices on the local network. Supports Zigbee, Z-Wave, WiFi, and Thread protocols.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Scan local network for compatible devices
- Step-by-step pairing wizard
- Device naming and room assignment
- Connection health monitoring
